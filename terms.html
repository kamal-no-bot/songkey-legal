What Quint does

Quint reads the key, the mode, the harmonic code, the tempo, the sections and the loudness of an audio file you own, of a file shared into it, or of twenty seconds captured from a speaker. It reports what it measured. It does not modify your audio and it does not create music.

Accuracy

Key and tempo detection is an estimate, not a fact. Quint reports the framework's own confidence and how much of the track stays in the key it names, and a track that changes key, a live recording or a heavily processed mix can all produce a reading a musician would disagree with. A twenty second capture from a room is the least reliable case of all, because the room is in the recording. Listen before you trust a number, and treat every reading as a starting point rather than an answer.

Loudness

Loudness is measured to ITU-R BS.1770 and compared against targets published by streaming platforms. Each target carries the page it was read from and the date it was checked. Those pages are published by the platforms and can change without notice, so check the source before you master to a number. Quint is a meter, not a mastering service, and a passing reading is not a guarantee that a platform will leave your track alone.

Music you do not own

Analyze audio you have the right to analyze. Apple Music streaming tracks are protected and Quint refuses them by design. Nothing in the app is a way around any protection.

The harmonic code

The 1A to 12B convention is a way of numbering keys that is common across DJ software. Quint implements the convention. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any DJ software vendor.
